K-Map Interactive Session

From Zero to Expert

5 levels × 3 questions. Each question is worth 10 marks. Total: 150.

  1. Beginner: 2-variable maps
  2. Easy: 3-variable maps, simple groups
  3. Medium: 3-variable maps, more groups
  4. Hard: 4-variable maps
  5. Expert: 4-variable maps with wrap-around and corners, plus a POS question

Marking per question: groups cover every 1 (2) · groups as large as possible (2) · no extra groups (1) · equation correct (3) · equation minimal (2).
